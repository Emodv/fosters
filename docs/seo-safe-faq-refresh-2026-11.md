## Redirect recovery checklist
1. Export GSC 'Page with redirect' examples with source URL, final destination, status and canonical.
2. Keep legitimate 301/308 canonical and migrated redirects; do not count them as errors.
3. Flag only: redirect-to-homepage without equivalent content, chains, loops, 302 used for permanent move, or destination 404/403.
4. For historically valuable articles, restore the same URL with relevant content where appropriate, or redirect to the closest equivalent resource, not automatically to the homepage.
5. Update internal links to final canonical destinations, sitemap entries and hreflang if applicable.
6. Validate HTTP status, canonical, indexability, internal links and GSC URL inspection after publication.
